# RTHC Coordinator User Manual

## 1. Introduction

RTHC (Real-Time Head Count) is used by coordinators to record workforce requirements, filled positions, vacancies, and daily headcount information for their assigned company and location.

Accurate and timely reporting matters because it supports manpower planning, vacancy tracking, daily operational decisions, and workforce visibility for the Head Office.

This manual is for RTHC Coordinator users only. It does not cover Super Admin functions such as user management, company administration, or system settings.

## 2. Accessing the Website

1. Open Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or another modern browser.
2. Enter the official RTHC website address.
3. Confirm the page belongs to the DMCFS RTHC platform.
4. Open the coordinator login page.

Use the website on a desktop computer, laptop, mobile phone, or tablet. The system is accessed through the browser.

## 3. Coordinator Login

1. Open the login page.
2. Enter the user ID or email provided by Head Office.
3. Enter the assigned password.
4. Use the password visibility control if needed.
5. Click Sign In.
6. Wait for the coordinator dashboard to load.

Important:
- Login credentials are issued by Head Office.
- Do not share your user ID or password with other employees.
- If you cannot log in, contact Head Office before continuing.

---

## 4. Verify Your Coordinator Information

After login, verify the information visible in the dashboard header before submitting data.

Check:
- Coordinator name
- Assigned company
- Assigned location

Example shown in the dashboard:
- Coordinator: Ganesh Abhang
- Company: Chakan Field Officer
- Location: Chakan MIDC

If the company or location is incorrect, contact Head Office before submitting any data.

---

## 5. Select the Scheme

1. Locate the Scheme dropdown.
2. Click the dropdown.
3. Review the available schemes.
4. Select the scheme applicable to the assigned workforce.
5. Confirm the selected scheme before continuing.

Examples of schemes shown in the project include:
- BVOC
- DVOC
- NAPS
- NATS
- WILP

Coordinate with the assigned work requirement so the correct scheme is selected before entering daily headcount details.

---

## 6. Select the Shift and Enter Headcount

1. Locate the Today's Head Count form.
2. Find the Shift dropdown.
3. Select the appropriate shift.
4. Choose Morning when reporting the morning shift.
5. Select the correct alternative shift when applicable.
6. Enter the workforce requirement.
7. Enter the number of positions filled.
8. Add optional remarks if needed.
9. Review the data before submitting.

Example fields:
- Shift: Morning / Mid / Night
- Requirement: Total workforce required
- Filled: Number of positions already occupied
- Remarks: Optional notes for the day

---

## 7. Submit the Daily Headcount

After reviewing the form, click Submit Head Count.

The system will save the data and show a confirmation message when the submission is successful. Always verify that the confirmation appears before moving to the next task.

If the confirmation does not appear or the data is incorrect, review the form and contact Head Office if needed.

---

## 8. Verify Submission Confirmation

After clicking Submit Head Count:
- Check that the confirmation popup appears.
- Verify that the system confirms the successful submission.
- Keep the confirmation as proof of completed reporting.

Do not assume the form was saved unless the confirmation message displays.

---

## 9. Review Previous Entries

Use the previous entries section to review your submitted records.

Check:
- Date of submission
- Requirement value
- Filled value
- Shift used
- Remarks recorded

Reviewing previous entries helps confirm that figures are accurate and that no duplicate or conflicting headcount updates were submitted.

## 10. Support and Reporting Problems

If you face a problem with the RTHC coordinator workflow, report it immediately to Head Office.

Common issues to report:
- Login failure
- Forgotten password
- Incorrect company or location
- Wrong scheme selected
- Incorrect headcount values
- Submission errors or missing confirmation

## 11. Important Reminder

Do not share your user ID or password.
Do not submit data for a company or location that is not assigned to you.
Do not leave the form incomplete or unverified.

## 9. Security and Compliance

- Do not share login details with anyone.
- Log out at the end of each session.
- Use only authorized work devices for system access.
- Maintain confidentiality of workforce and operational data.
- Report suspected unauthorized access immediately.
